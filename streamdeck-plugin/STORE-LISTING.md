# Elgato Marketplace listing — NE Broadcast Suite

## Tagline (short summary)

Run your entire esports broadcast from your Stream Deck — scores, graphics, countdowns, replays and your run of show, all live on your keys.

## Description

Turn your Stream Deck into a broadcast control surface for NE Broadcast Suite, the esports production studio for Rocket League, Valorant, CS2, League of Legends, Overwatch, Marvel Rivals and more.

Every key is live. Score keys show the real series score in your team's colors. Scene keys light up when they're on program. The countdown key becomes a ticking clock. Your run of show displays the next segment by name before you press it. If the suite closes, every key grays out — and reconnects on its own the moment you're back.

**Scores and series**
- Add or remove series wins with one press — the key always shows the live score
- Set an exact score, bump the game number, swap teams
- Reset the series or clear the scoreboard, protected by hold-to-fire so a stray tap can't wipe a live match
- Put any saved team on either side: name, logo, colors and roster in one press

**Graphics and overlays**
- Toggle any full-screen graphic on or off air: break, matchup, winner, line-up, map veto, bracket, standings, schedule, credits and more
- Optional autocut: switching a graphic live can also cut OBS to a paired scene, and return when it hides
- Stack the persistent overlays independently: ticker, casters desk, sponsor banner, spotlights
- One panic key takes every graphic off air

**Countdowns**
- Start, pause, stop, reset, show/hide, and add or subtract time on the fly
- A dedicated Countdown Clock key ticks the remaining time every second, in your choice of format
- One-press break headlines: STARTING SOON, BE RIGHT BACK, UP NEXT, TECHNICAL DIFFICULTIES, or your own
- Durations accept minutes and seconds ("5:00", "2m30s") — no math required

**Run of show**
- Step through your rundown from the deck: next, back, re-run current, end show
- Keys display the actual segment names and your position, live
- Drive the Desk Show topics rail: next topic on the key face, with per-topic scene cuts

**Production**
- Cut program scenes through the suite's switcher (OBS or vMix)
- Fire manual Flows, force HUD or scoreboard view
- Mute audio inputs (keys light red while muted), show or hide sources
- Save the OBS replay buffer, toggle it, or create a Twitch clip and an OBS clip in one press
- Drop Twitch stream markers, and run ad breaks with a hold-to-start safety

No configuration needed: the plugin finds NE Broadcast Suite automatically on the same PC, and every dropdown fills itself with your real scenes, flows, teams and audio inputs.

Requires NE Broadcast Suite for Windows. Get it at https://www.namelessesports.com

## Review notes (for the submission form, if asked)

This plugin is a companion controller for the NE Broadcast Suite desktop application and communicates with it over a local WebSocket (localhost only, no external network access). Key images update at most once per 150 ms; the countdown clock updates once per second.
